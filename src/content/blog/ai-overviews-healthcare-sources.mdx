**Google AI Overviews appear before any organic result.** When a patient searches for a healthcare provider, AI Overviews synthesize information and present a summary at the top of the page. Being cited in that overview is the new first page of Google.

## What Are Google AI Overviews?

AI Overviews are Google's AI-generated summaries that appear at the top of search results. They synthesize information from multiple sources to provide a direct answer to the user's query.

For healthcare queries, AI Overviews pull from:

- **Your website** — Structured content, schema markup, service pages
- **Google Business Profile** — Your verified business listing
- **Healthcare directories** — Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Vitals
- **Authoritative sources** — Medical journals, hospital systems, professional organizations

## How AI Overviews Choose Sources

Google's AI Overviews prioritize sources based on three factors:

**1. Relevance.** Does your content directly answer the query? A page about "dermatologist in Austin" is more relevant than a generic "about us" page.

**2. Authority.** Does Google trust your source? Verified Google Business Profile listings, professional directory entries, and authoritative backlinks all signal trust.

**3. Structure.** Is your content easy for AI to extract? Clear headings, concise definitions, and structured data make it easier for AI Overviews to pull your information.

## What Healthcare Practices Should Do

- **Optimize your Google Business Profile.** Complete every field. Add services, specialties, photos, and posts. Verify your listing.
- **Add schema markup.** `MedicalOrganization`, `Physician`, `Service` schema tells AI exactly what you are.
- **Create question-answer content.** Service pages that answer "What is [treatment]?" followed by a direct definition.
- **Ensure directory consistency.** Your information must match across all platforms AI pulls from.

## How to Check Your AI Overview Presence

Search Google for "[your specialty] in [your city]" and see if an AI Overview appears. If it does, check which sources it cites. If your practice isn't among them, you have work to do.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I pay to appear in Google AI Overviews?

No. AI Overviews are generated based on content quality and relevance, not advertising. The practices that appear are the ones whose data is most relevant, authoritative, and well-structured.

### How often do AI Overviews change?

AI Overviews update regularly as Google's algorithms improve. The key is having consistent, structured information that remains accurate over time.

### Do AI Overviews replace traditional search results?

No. AI Overviews appear alongside traditional results. But they capture the user's attention first — being cited in the overview means being seen before any organic result.
