**AI search doesn't care about your Google ranking.** It cares about data quality, consistency, and structure. If your practice's information isn't formatted for AI consumption, AI won't mention you — regardless of how many backlinks you have.

## The AI Search Reality

Patients are increasingly asking AI tools instead of Googling:

- "Which dermatologist accepts Aetna near me?"
- "Who's the best orthopedic surgeon in [city]?"
- "What fertility clinic has the highest success rates?"

AI gives one answer. If your practice isn't in that answer, you don't exist to that patient.

## The Five Things AI Needs to Mention You

**1. Structured data on your website.** Schema markup that tells AI: this is a medical practice, this is what we treat, this is where we are, these are our doctors. Without this, AI has to guess.

**2. Consistent directory listings.** Your name, address, phone, and specialties must match exactly across Google Business Profile, Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Vitals, and your website. One difference breaks the chain.

**3. Clear specialty definitions.** Don't just say "doctor." Say "board-certified dermatologist specializing in Mohs surgery and cosmetic dermatology." AI needs specificity.

**4. Verifiable credentials.** Your doctors should be listed in professional directories — AMA, specialty boards, hospital affiliations. AI cross-references these to verify credibility.

**5. Authoritative content.** Service pages that answer patient questions directly. FAQs that mirror how patients actually ask questions. Content structured for extraction, not just human reading.

## How to Start Today

1. Search your practice on Google Business Profile, Healthgrades, and Zocdoc. Are the details identical?
2. Check your website for schema markup. Does it include `MedicalOrganization` or `Physician` schema?
3. Ask an AI tool about your specialty in your area. Does your practice come up?

If any of these have gaps, that's where to start.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does it take to get mentioned in AI search?

Initial improvements in data consistency can be indexed within 2-4 weeks. Building full AI visibility — structured data, cross-platform consistency, authority signals — typically takes 3-6 months.

### Do I need to change my website to be visible in AI search?

You need to add structured data (schema markup), ensure consistent information across all platforms, and create content that answers patient questions directly. The website itself doesn't need a complete redesign.

### Is AI search replacing Google for healthcare?

Not replacing, but supplementing. Patients still use Google, but an increasing number are using AI tools for healthcare research. The practices that optimize for both will have the strongest visibility.
