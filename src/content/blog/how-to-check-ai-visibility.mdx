### Platform 1: ChatGPT (Bing Index)

ChatGPT uses [Bing's index, not Google](https://www.storefrontaudit.com/blog/is-your-business-invisible-to-chatgpt). If your sitemap isn't submitted to Bing Webmaster Tools, ChatGPT can't find you.

#### Prompt 1: Identity Check

> "Who is [your name]? Are they a [specialty] doctor in [city]?"

**What to look for:**

- ✅ AI recognizes you by name
- ✅ Correct specialty and location
- ✅ Correct credentials
- ❌ Confuses you with someone else
- ❌ Reports no information
- ❌ Returns wrong information

#### Prompt 2: Recommendation Check

> "Who should I see for [specific service] in [city]?"

**What to look for:**

- ✅ Your practice is recommended by name
- ✅ AI states a reason (specialty, reviews, credentials)
- ❌ AI hedges ("I can't recommend specific providers")
- ❌ AI redirects to generic directories
- ❌ Your competitors are named, you're not

#### Prompt 3: Comparison Check

> "What's the difference between [your practice] and [competitor] in [city]?"

**What to look for:**

- ✅ AI has information about both practices
- ✅ Information is accurate
- ❌ AI only knows the competitor
- ❌ AI returns wrong information

---

### Platform 2: Perplexity (Real-Time Crawl)

Perplexity does [real-time crawling](https://www.storefrontaudit.com/blog/is-your-business-invisible-to-chatgpt), unlike ChatGPT which relies on Bing's index. It favors content with statistics and structured data.

#### Prompt 4: Direct Search

> "[Your practice name] [city]"

**What to look for:**

- ✅ Your website appears in sources
- ✅ Information is accurate
- ❌ Your website doesn't appear
- ❌ Wrong information is cited

#### Prompt 5: Service Search

> "Best [specialty] in [city] for [specific service]"

**What to look for:**

- ✅ Your practice is mentioned
- ✅ Cited as a source
- ❌ Competitors are cited, you're not

#### Prompt 6: Review Check

> "What do patients say about [your practice name]?"

**What to look for:**

- ✅ Accurate review summary
- ✅ Positive sentiment
- ❌ No information available
- ❌ Negative sentiment from wrong sources

---

### Platform 3: Google AI Overviews (Google Index)

Google AI Overviews pull from Google's index and Google Business Profile data. According to [BrightEdge (February 2026)](https://www.brightedge.com/resources/research-reports/ai-overviews-report), AI Overviews trigger on 88% of healthcare queries.

#### Prompt 7: Local Search

> "[Specialty] near me" or "[Specialty] in [city]"

**What to look for:**

- ✅ AI Overview appears
- ✅ Your practice is mentioned in the overview
- ❌ AI Overview appears but you're not mentioned
- ❌ No AI Overview appears

#### Prompt 8: Question Search

> "How do I choose a [specialty] in [city]?"

**What to look for:**

- ✅ Your practice is cited in the overview
- ✅ Your content is referenced
- ❌ Competitors are cited, you're not

---

### Platform 4: Claude (Web Search)

Claude values content depth, accuracy, and authoritative sources. It searches the web less frequently than other platforms but rewards well-structured, detailed content.

#### Prompt 9: Direct Question

> "Tell me about [your practice name] in [city]"

**What to look for:**

- ✅ Accurate information about your practice
- ✅ Correct services and specialties
- ❌ No information available
- ❌ Wrong information

#### Prompt 10: Recommendation

> "Who are the best [specialty] doctors in [city]?"

**What to look for:**

- ✅ Your practice is mentioned
- ✅ AI gives a reason for the recommendation
- ❌ Not mentioned at all
- ❌ Only competitors are mentioned

## Platform-Specific Fixes

### Fix ChatGPT Visibility (Bing Index) — 30 Minutes

ChatGPT uses [Bing's index, not Google](https://www.storefrontaudit.com/blog/is-your-business-invisible-to-chatgpt). If your sitemap isn't submitted to Bing, ChatGPT can't find you.

**Steps:**

1. Go to [bing.com/webmasters](https://www.bing.com/webmasters)
2. Sign in with a Microsoft account
3. Add your site: `https://www.pagerstudio.space`
4. Submit your sitemap: `https://www.pagerstudio.space/sitemap.xml`
5. Request indexing for your homepage
6. Wait 2–4 weeks for Bing to process

**Expected impact:** ChatGPT visibility within 2–4 weeks.

### Fix Perplexity Visibility (Real-Time Crawl) — 1–2 Hours

Perplexity crawls your site in real-time. It needs crawlable, structured content with [statistics and data points](https://www.storefrontaudit.com/blog/is-your-business-invisible-to-chatgpt).

**Steps:**

1. Ensure your site allows all crawlers (check `robots.txt`)
2. Add structured data: `MedicalOrganization`, `Physician`, `FAQPage` schema
3. Add statistics and external citations to your content
4. Use question-answer format in your content
5. Add clear headings that match patient queries

**Expected impact:** Perplexity visibility within 1–2 weeks.

### Fix Google AI Overview Visibility (Google Index) — 1–2 Hours

Google AI Overviews pull from [Google's index + GBP data](https://www.brightedge.com/resources/research-reports/ai-overviews-report). Pages with valid schema earn [67% higher citation eligibility](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/aeo-best-practices-2026).

**Steps:**

1. Claim and complete your Google Business Profile (100%)
2. Add all services, specialties, and locations
3. Add `FAQPage` schema to your FAQ section
4. Add question-based headings to your content
5. Upload photos weekly to GBP
6. Respond to all reviews

**Expected impact:** Google AI Overview visibility within 2–4 weeks.

### Fix Claude Visibility (Content Quality) — 2–4 Hours

Claude values depth, accuracy, and authoritative content. It rewards [well-structured, detailed pages](https://www.lseo.com/generative-engine-optimization-geo-for-healthcare-and-medical-companies).

**Steps:**

1. Add detailed service pages (500+ words each)
2. Add credentials, experience, and training details
3. Add case studies or patient testimonials
4. Add external citations to medical sources
5. Ensure content uses definition-first sentence structure

**Expected impact:** Claude visibility within 4–8 weeks.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I check if my practice shows up in AI search?

Run this 20-minute test: Ask 10 prompts across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI, and Claude. Use the prompt matrix in this guide. Document results in a spreadsheet. If your practice appears with correct information, you're visible. If not, you need to fix your Bing indexation and structured data.

### Why doesn't my practice show up in ChatGPT?

ChatGPT uses Bing's index, not Google. If your sitemap isn't submitted to Bing Webmaster Tools, ChatGPT can't find you. Also check: schema markup, directory consistency, and GBP completeness. According to [Storefront Audit](https://www.storefrontaudit.com/blog/is-your-business-invisible-to-chatgpt), this is the #1 reason practices are invisible to ChatGPT.

### Why doesn't my practice show up in Google AI Overviews?

Google AI Overviews pull from Google's index + GBP data. Complete your GBP profile, add `FAQPage` schema, and ensure your content has question-answer format. According to [BrightEdge (February 2026)](https://www.brightedge.com/resources/research-reports/ai-overviews-report), AI Overviews trigger on 88% of healthcare queries — if you're not appearing, your data isn't structured for extraction.

### Why doesn't my practice show up in Perplexity?

Perplexity crawls your site in real-time. Ensure your site allows all crawlers, has structured data, and contains content with clear question-answer format. Perplexity favors [content with statistics and data points](https://www.storefrontaudit.com/blog/is-your-business-invisible-to-chatgpt).

### How often should I check my AI visibility?

Monthly. AI engines take 4–12 weeks to process changes. Weekly checks produce noise, not signal. Check monthly across all four platforms and document your results.

### What's the fastest way to improve AI visibility?

Fix Bing indexation first (30 minutes). Then complete your Google Business Profile (1 hour). Then add schema markup (2–4 hours). These three fixes address 80% of visibility issues according to [Stackmatix research](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/aeo-best-practices-2026).

### Can I pay to appear in AI recommendations?

No. AI recommendations are algorithmically generated based on data quality, consistency, and authority. You cannot buy placement. The practices that appear are the ones whose data is most structured and trustworthy.

### What if my practice appears but with wrong information?

This is worse than being invisible. Wrong information erodes trust. Fix the source the AI is reading from — usually a directory listing or old webpage. Update the source, then re-check in 4–6 weeks.
