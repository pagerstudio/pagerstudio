**SEO gets you ranked. AEO gets you recommended.** That single difference changes everything about how healthcare practices should approach their online visibility.

## What Is SEO?

Search Engine Optimization (SEO) is the process of optimizing your website to rank higher in Google's search results. It focuses on keywords, backlinks, page speed, mobile-friendliness, and content quality.

When a patient Googles "dermatologist near me," SEO determines which practices appear in the top ten results.

## What Is AEO?

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the process of structuring your online presence so that AI systems — ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews — can find, understand, and recommend your practice.

When a patient asks ChatGPT "who's the best dermatologist in [city]?", AEO determines whether AI includes your practice in its answer.

## The Key Differences

| Factor | SEO | AEO |
|--------|-----|-----|
| Goal | Rank in search results | Be cited in AI answers |
| Mechanism | Keywords, backlinks, page speed | Structured data, consistency, clarity |
| Success metric | Traffic from search | Mentions in AI responses |
| Content style | Keyword-rich, long-form | Direct, extractable, well-structured |
| User behavior | Click through multiple results | Receive one AI-generated answer |

## Why Healthcare Needs Both

A healthcare practice can rank #1 on Google and still be invisible to AI. Here's why:

- **SEO relies on links.** AI doesn't count links the same way — it cross-references multiple sources to verify facts.
- **SEO optimizes for clicks.** AI doesn't click — it extracts and synthesizes.
- **SEO rewards content volume.** AI rewards content clarity and structure.

The practices that dominate in 2026 will do both: rank in Google AND be recommended by AI.

## How to Know If You Need AEO

Ask yourself: when a patient asks ChatGPT about your specialty in your area, does AI mention your practice? If the answer is no, you need AEO — regardless of how strong your SEO is.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I do AEO without SEO?

Technically yes, but you shouldn't. SEO and AEO complement each other. Strong SEO helps your website rank in Google. Strong AEO helps AI recommend your practice. Most healthcare practices need both.

### Which is more important for healthcare?

AEO is becoming more important as AI search adoption grows. But SEO still drives significant traffic. The best strategy is to do both — optimize for Google AND for AI.

### How long does AEO take compared to SEO?

Initial AEO improvements (data consistency, structured data) can be indexed within weeks. Full AEO visibility typically takes 3-6 months — similar to SEO. Both compound over time.
