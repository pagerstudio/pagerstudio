**Dental implant patients research extensively before choosing a provider.** They ask AI questions like "which dentist specializes in dental implants?" or "who's the best implant dentist near me?" If your practice isn't structured for AI to find and recommend, you lose patients to competitors who are.

## Why Dental Implants Need AEO

Dental implants are high-cost, high-consideration procedures. Patients compare success rates, doctor credentials, technology, and reviews — often using AI tools before they ever visit a website.

When a patient asks ChatGPT "who should I see for dental implants in [city]?", the AI doesn't return ten links. It gives one answer. AEO determines whether that answer includes your practice.

## What AI Sees When It Looks at Your Practice

AI pulls information from four sources:

- **Your website** — Services, specialties, doctor bios, technology
- **Directory listings** — Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Google Business Profile
- **Third-party mentions** — News articles, reviews, professional directories
- **Structured data** — Schema markup that tells AI exactly what your practice is

If your implant success rates are on your website but not in your schema markup, AI might miss them. If your phone number differs between Healthgrades and your website, AI might not connect the two listings.

## The Three Things Dental Implant Practices Must Fix

**1. Structured data.** Add `MedicalOrganization` or `Dentist` schema with your specialties, locations, and services. Include `MedicalProcedure` for implant placement, bone grafting, and sinus lifts.

**2. Directory consistency.** Your practice name, address, phone, and specialties must match exactly across every platform. One difference breaks AI's trust chain.

**3. Extractable content.** Your service pages should answer questions directly. "What are dental implants?" followed by a clear definition. "What is your implant success rate?" followed by specific numbers.

## How to Start

Ask an AI tool: "Which dental implant practices do you recommend in [your city]?" If your practice isn't mentioned, AI doesn't know you — or doesn't trust your data enough to recommend you.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is AEO for dental practices?

AEO for dental practices is the process of structuring your practice's online presence so that AI tools — ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity — can find, understand, and recommend your practice when patients ask dental-related questions.

### How do dental implant patients use AI?

Dental implant patients ask AI about success rates, procedure details, doctor credentials, and practice comparisons. AI synthesizes information from multiple sources and gives one recommendation — not a list of links.

### What makes dental AEO different from regular SEO?

SEO focuses on ranking in Google. AEO focuses on being cited in AI answers. A dental practice can rank #1 on Google and still be invisible to AI if its data isn't structured for AI consumption.
