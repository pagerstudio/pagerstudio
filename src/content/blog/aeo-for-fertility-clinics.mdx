**Fertility patients ask AI questions that require specific, trustworthy answers.** "Which fertility clinic specializes in recurrent pregnancy loss?" "Who's the best reproductive endocrinologist in [city]?" AI doesn't guess — it recommends the clinic whose data is cleanest and most complete.

## Why Fertility Clinics Are Uniquely Affected

Fertility treatment is deeply personal and highly researched. Patients compare clinics across multiple dimensions: success rates, doctor credentials, treatment options, insurance acceptance, and location. AI excels at synthesizing this complexity — but only if the data is structured for it.

The fertility clinics that win in AI search are the ones that make their information easy for AI to extract, verify, and trust.

## What AI Needs to Recommend Your Clinic

AI builds its recommendation from structured signals:

- **Specialty clarity** — Does your website explicitly say "fertility clinic" and "reproductive endocrinology"?
- **Service specificity** — Do you list IVF, IUI, egg freezing, genetic testing, male fertility, recurrent pregnancy loss?
- **Credential verification** — Are your doctors listed in professional directories with matching credentials?
- **Location accuracy** — Does every listing show the same address, same phone, same hours?

A clinic that treats "fertility issues" but doesn't specify "recurrent pregnancy loss," "male factor infertility," or "preimplantation genetic testing" will be outranked by a competitor that does.

## The Fertility AEO Checklist

1. **Schema markup** — Add `MedicalOrganization` with `MedicalSpecialty: Reproductive Endocrinology`
2. **Service pages** — Individual pages for each treatment with clear definitions
3. **Directory sync** — Match your Google Business Profile, Healthgrades, and Zocdoc exactly
4. **FAQ content** — Answer the questions patients actually ask AI
5. **Author bios** — Doctor pages with credentials, specialties, and professional affiliations

## How to Check Your Current Visibility

Ask ChatGPT, Gemini, or Perplexity: "Which fertility clinics do you recommend in [your city]?" If your clinic isn't mentioned, you have an AI visibility problem.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is AEO for fertility clinics?

AEO for fertility clinics is the practice of structuring your clinic's online presence so AI tools can find, understand, and recommend your practice when patients ask fertility-related questions.

### How do fertility patients use AI?

Fertility patients ask AI about success rates, treatment options, doctor credentials, and clinic comparisons. AI synthesizes information from multiple sources and gives one recommendation — not a list of links.

### What's the difference between SEO and AEO for fertility?

SEO gets your website ranked in Google results. AEO gets your clinic recommended in AI answers. A fertility clinic can rank #1 on Google and still be invisible to AI search.
