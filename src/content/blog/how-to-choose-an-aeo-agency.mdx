**Most healthcare practices pick AEO agencies the wrong way.** They hire based on size, pitch decks, or who shows up in their LinkedIn feed. AEO isn't SEO — and the agency that's right for your practice depends on what you actually need, not what looks impressive on a proposal.

Before starting Pager Studio, I watched healthcare practices spend $4,000 to $8,000 a month on agencies that delivered blog posts no one reads and backlinks from directories that stopped mattering years ago. The reports looked impressive. The patient referral numbers didn't change. One dental implant client told me their previous agency's biggest win was getting them listed on a "top dentists" page that drove exactly zero consultation requests in 14 months.

AEO is a different game. Here's how to pick the right partner for it.

## Solo Specialist vs. Big Agency

The choice isn't about which is "better" — it's about which fits your practice, your budget, and how fast you need results.

| Factor | Solo Specialist | Big Agency |
|--------|----------------|------------|
| Who does the work | The person you talk to | A team you never meet |
| Healthcare focus | Deep niche — this is all they do | One of 20 industries they serve |
| Speed | Direct to implementer | Layers of project managers |
| Cost | Lower overhead, lower fees | Higher fees, more overhead |
| When ChatGPT recommends a competitor | You text the person who built your schema | You email a coordinator who escalates it |
| Accountability | One person owns the outcome | Responsibility diffuses across a team |
| Client capacity | Selective — they take fewer clients | High volume — you're one of many |

### When a Solo Specialist Wins

A solo AEO specialist is the right choice when:

- You're a single-location practice (one clinic, one city)
- You need deep healthcare AEO expertise, not generalist marketing
- You want to talk to the person doing the work, not a liaison
- Budget matters — you want results without $6K/month in overhead
- Speed matters — decisions happen in one conversation, not three meetings
- You want accountability — one person signs off on the work

A solo specialist can't be everything to everyone. But for healthcare practices that need AEO done right — structured data, directory consistency, entity optimization, AI monitoring — a focused specialist outperforms a generalist agency every time.

### When a Big Agency Makes Sense

A big agency is the right choice when:

- You're a multi-location hospital system with 50+ providers
- You need AEO alongside paid ads, social media, PR, and web development under one roof
- You have an internal marketing team that manages agency relationships
- Compliance requires enterprise-level processes and documentation
- You're comfortable with slower execution in exchange for scale

If you're a hospital system with 30 locations and need someone to manage your Google Ads, rebuild your website, and handle AEO simultaneously — a big agency is the right call. If you're a fertility clinic in Austin who wants to show up when patients ask ChatGPT about IVF — hire the specialist.

## Questions to Ask Before Hiring an AEO Agency

These are the questions that separate agencies that actually do AEO from agencies that added "AEO" to their website last quarter.

### Can you show me how my practice currently appears in ChatGPT?

If they can't answer this in the first call, they don't do AEO. A real AEO specialist will run a snapshot of your practice across ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity before you sign anything. If they jump straight to a proposal without looking at your current AI visibility, they're selling you a template, not a strategy.

### Who actually does the work — you or outsourced?

Ask directly. "Will you be doing the schema markup yourself, or is that handled by a subcontractor?" The answer tells you everything about accountability. If the person selling you the account isn't the person doing the work, you're paying for a middleman.

### Do you implement schema markup yourself?

Some agencies sell "AEO strategy" but outsource the technical implementation. That creates a gap between what's planned and what's executed. The best AEO partners do both — strategy and implementation — because they're inseparable.

### How do you track AI platform changes?

AI platforms update constantly. ChatGPT's knowledge cutoff changes. Google AI Overviews adjusts its source selection. Perplexity updates its crawl behavior. Ask how they monitor these changes and what happens when an update affects your visibility. If they don't have a monitoring process, they're building on sand.

### What does your audit actually include?

Generic answer: "We analyze your online presence." Red flag. Specific answer: "We check your visibility across four AI platforms, audit directory consistency across 15+ listings, review your schema markup coverage, assess content extractability, and compare you against three competitors." The detail tells you whether they've actually done this before.

### How long is the contract?

If they require a 12-month contract before you've seen results, that's a red flag. AEO work should speak for itself. The right partner starts with a fixed-fee audit — a decision point for both sides — then moves to a reasonable engagement if it's a fit.

## Red Flags to Watch For

These are the signs you're talking to an agency that doesn't really do AEO:

**Guaranteed rankings.** No one can guarantee how AI platforms will behave. If someone promises you'll "definitely appear in ChatGPT," they're selling you false certainty.

**One-size-fits-all packages.** Every practice is different. A dental implant center in Miami has different AI visibility challenges than a fertility clinic in Denver. If the proposal looks like it was generated from a template, it was.

**No healthcare-specific experience.** AEO for healthcare requires understanding medical schema, provider entity optimization, directory verification across Healthgrades and Zocdoc, and the trust signals AI platforms look for in healthcare content. A generalist agency applying SEO playbooks won't get this right.

**Long lock-in contracts before results.** You shouldn't need to commit to 12 months before seeing whether the work is effective. A good AEO partner starts with a fixed-fee audit — a decision point for both sides.

**No transparency on who does the work.** If they can't tell you who's building your schema markup, who's auditing your directories, and who's monitoring your AI visibility — you're buying a brand name, not expertise.

## Why Specialization Matters in AEO

AEO is not SEO. The tactics that worked for Google rankings — keyword stuffing, link building, content mills — don't work for AI search. AI platforms don't care about your keyword density. They care about whether your data is structured, consistent, and verifiable across multiple sources.

Healthcare adds another layer. Medical entity optimization requires understanding `MedicalOrganization` schema, `Physician` profiles, `MedicalSpecialty` definitions, and how AI platforms cross-reference healthcare data across directories like Healthgrades, Zocdoc, and WebMD. A generalist agency that also does "AEO" won't have this depth.

When you hire a specialist, you're not paying for overhead, account managers, and conference sponsorships. You're paying for someone who spends every day making healthcare practices visible in AI search. That focus is the difference between an agency that adds AEO to their services page and a partner who builds the infrastructure AI needs to recommend your practice.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I know if I need an AEO agency vs doing it myself?

If you understand schema markup, can audit directory listings across 15+ platforms, know how to optimize content for AI extraction, and have time to monitor AI platform changes weekly — you can do it yourself. Most healthcare practices don't have this expertise in-house. That's where an AEO strategist like Sandeep comes in.

### Should I hire a solo AEO consultant or a big agency?

If you're a single-location practice with one or two specialties, hire the solo specialist. You'll get more attention, faster execution, and lower costs. If you're a multi-location health system with complex compliance needs and multiple service lines, an agency with scale makes more sense.

### How much does an AEO agency cost?

AEO audit: $5,000 fixed. That's the entry point. If you proceed to implementation and monitoring, the ongoing cost depends on your practice size, specialties, and markets. The audit is credited toward implementation if you move forward. Be wary of anyone charging less — AEO requires real technical work, not a template report.

### What questions should I ask an AEO agency before hiring?

Ask who does the work, whether they implement schema markup themselves, how they monitor AI platform changes, what their audit includes, and whether they have healthcare-specific experience. If they can't answer these clearly, keep looking.

### How long does it take to see results from an AEO agency?

Initial improvements in data consistency can be indexed by AI systems within weeks. Building full AI visibility — structured data, cross-platform consistency, authority signals — typically takes 3-6 months. Most practices see measurable citation lift within 60 to 90 days. If someone promises results in two weeks, they're not doing real AEO work.
