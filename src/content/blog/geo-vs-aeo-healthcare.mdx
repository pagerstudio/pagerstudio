**GEO and AEO are often used interchangeably, but they're not the same thing.** Understanding the difference helps healthcare practices allocate their efforts more effectively.

## What Is GEO?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the broader practice of optimizing content to perform well in AI-generated responses. It focuses on making your content authoritative, well-structured, and citation-worthy so that AI systems reference and include it in generated answers.

GEO is about being a source that AI trusts and cites.

## What Is AEO?

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is a subset of GEO focused specifically on being the answer AI gives when a user asks a direct question. It's about structured data, consistent directory presence, and extractable content — the technical foundation that makes AI recommend your practice.

AEO is about being the recommendation AI makes.

## How They Overlap

Both GEO and AEO aim to make your practice visible in AI-powered search. They share common tactics:

- Structured data and schema markup
- Clear, extractable content
- Consistent information across platforms
- Authoritative, trustworthy content

## Where They Differ

| Aspect | GEO | AEO |
|--------|-----|-----|
| Focus | Being cited as a source | Being recommended as an answer |
| Tactics | Content quality, authority signals | Schema markup, directory consistency |
| Scope | All AI-generated content | Direct questions and recommendations |
| Measurement | Citation frequency | Recommendation frequency |

## What Healthcare Practices Need

For most healthcare practices, AEO is the priority. You want AI to recommend your practice when patients ask questions. GEO becomes important as you build authority — getting cited in AI-generated articles, research summaries, and industry content.

Start with AEO. Layer GEO on top as your authority grows.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is GEO the same as AEO?

No. GEO is the broader practice of optimizing for AI-generated content. AEO is a subset focused specifically on being the answer AI gives. AEO is more tactical; GEO is more strategic.

### Which should healthcare practices focus on first?

AEO. It provides the technical foundation — structured data, directory consistency, extractable content. Once that's solid, layer GEO tactics to build authority and citation frequency.

### Will GEO replace AEO?

No. They serve different purposes and complement each other. AEO ensures AI can find and recommend you. GEO ensures AI cites and references you. Both matter.
