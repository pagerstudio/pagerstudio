**IVF patients research more than almost any other healthcare specialty.** They ask AI tools questions like "which IVF clinic has the best success rates?" or "who's the best fertility specialist near me?" If your clinic isn't structured for AI to find and recommend, you lose patients to competitors who are.

## Why IVF Clinics Need AEO

IVF is a high-consideration, high-cost decision. Patients don't pick the first clinic they find. They research success rates, doctor credentials, treatment options, and reviews — often using AI tools before they ever visit a website.

When a patient asks ChatGPT "which IVF clinic should I choose in [city]?", the AI doesn't return ten links. It gives one answer. AEO determines whether that answer includes your clinic.

## What AI Sees When It Looks at Your Clinic

AI pulls information from four sources to build its recommendation:

- **Your website** — Services, specialties, doctor bios, success rates
- **Directory listings** — Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Google Business Profile
- **Third-party mentions** — News articles, reviews, professional directories
- **Structured data** — Schema markup that tells AI exactly what your clinic is

If your success rates are on your website but not in your schema markup, AI might miss them. If your phone number differs between Healthgrades and your website, AI might not connect the two listings.

## The Three Things IVF Clinics Must Fix

**1. Structured data.** Add `MedicalOrganization` or `Physician` schema with your specialties, locations, and services. Include `MedicalProcedure` schema for IVF, IUI, egg freezing, and other treatments.

**2. Directory consistency.** Your clinic name, address, phone, and specialties must match exactly across every platform. One difference breaks AI's trust chain.

**3. Extractable content.** Your service pages should answer questions directly. "What is IVF?" followed by a clear definition. "What are your success rates?" followed by specific numbers. AI extracts facts, not marketing copy.

## How to Start

Ask an AI tool: "Which IVF clinics do you recommend in [your city]?" If your clinic isn't mentioned, AI doesn't know you — or doesn't trust your data enough to recommend you.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is AEO for IVF clinics?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) for IVF clinics is the process of structuring your clinic's online presence so that AI tools — ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity — can find, understand, and recommend your clinic when patients ask fertility-related questions.

### How do IVF patients use AI to find clinics?

IVF patients increasingly ask AI tools questions like "which IVF clinic has the best success rates?" or "who specializes in IVF near me?" instead of Googling. AI gives them one answer — not ten links.

### What makes IVF AEO different from regular SEO?

SEO focuses on ranking in Google. AEO focuses on being cited in AI answers. IVF clinics can rank #1 on Google and still be invisible to AI if their data isn't structured for AI consumption.
