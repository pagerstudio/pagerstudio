Google AI Overviews is no longer a feature. It is the default. Every time a patient searches for a healthcare topic on Google, an AI-generated overview appears at the top of the results — often before any organic listings.

For healthcare practices, this changes the fundamental equation of search visibility. Ranking #1 on Google is no longer enough. You need to be cited in the AI Overview itself.

## How Google AI Overviews Works

When a patient searches "best dermatologist in Mumbai" on Google, the page now shows:

1. **AI Overview**: A paragraph generated by Google's AI, citing specific sources
2. **Sponsored results**: Ads
3. **Local pack**: Google Maps results
4. **Organic results**: Traditional blue links

The AI Overview occupies the most prominent position on the page. It names specific practices, cites sources, and provides a direct answer to the patient's question. Patients who read the AI Overview may never scroll to the organic results.

## What Determines AI Overview Citations

Google AI Overviews pulls from sources it considers authoritative, well-structured, and directly relevant to the search query. The citation criteria are:

- **Structured data**: Pages with medical schema markup are significantly more likely to be cited than pages without
- **Content depth**: Pages that directly answer the search query in clear, specific language
- **Authority signals**: Pages from domains with established trust and expertise in healthcare
- **Freshness**: Recently updated content is preferred over outdated information
- **Consistency**: Information that matches across your website, Google Business Profile, and directory listings

The single most controllable factor is structured data. Adding medical schema markup to your website directly increases your chances of being cited in Google AI Overviews.

## The Healthcare Specificity Problem

Google AI Overviews treats healthcare content under YMYL (Your Money, Your Life) standards. This means it applies stricter quality criteria than for other topics. Specifically:

- **Named expertise**: AI Overview citations favor pages that identify specific physicians and their credentials, not generic "our team" content
- **Medical specificity**: Pages that name specific procedures, conditions, and treatments rather than general wellness advice
- **Citation quality**: Pages that reference authoritative medical sources and have citations from trusted health directories

Most healthcare practice websites fail on all three counts. They use generic language, avoid naming specific doctors, and lack the structured data that AI Overview requires.

## What Changed With the June 2026 Update

Google's most recent update to AI Overviews expanded healthcare coverage significantly. Key changes:

- **More healthcare queries trigger AI Overviews**: Previously, only broad queries like "best cardiologist" triggered AI Overviews. Now, specific queries like "cost of angioplasty in Delhi" and "LASIK vs SMILE comparison" also trigger them.
- **More specific citations**: AI Overviews now cites specific procedures, doctors, and outcomes rather than just practice names
- **Local context**: AI Overviews increasingly includes location-specific information, citing practices in the patient's city or region
- **Competitor comparison**: AI Overviews now directly compares practices, naming specific advantages and differentiators

This means healthcare practices cannot just be present — they need to be specific, structured, and locally relevant.

## How to Optimize for Google AI Overviews

The optimization strategy has four components:

### 1. Schema Markup

Add MedicalClinic, Physician, and MedicalProcedure schema to your website. This is the foundation. Without it, Google AI cannot reliably identify your practice, doctors, or procedures.

### 2. Content Specificity

Replace generic content with specific, structured information. Instead of "we offer advanced cardiac care," write "Dr. [Name] performs coronary angioplasty using the [specific catheter platform] with a success rate of [specific metric]."

### 3. FAQ Structure

Create FAQ content that directly answers the questions patients ask on Google. Structure each FAQ with the exact question as the heading and a clear, specific answer. This format is what Google AI Overviews pulls from.

### 4. Directory Consistency

Ensure your practice name, address, phone number, and doctor names are identical across Google Business Profile, Practo, Justdial, and all other directories. Inconsistency causes AI to distrust your information.

## The Measurable Impact

Healthcare practices that implement these four components typically see:

- **40-60% increase** in AI Overview citations within 90 days
- **2-3x more click-throughs** from AI Overview citations than from organic blue links
- **Higher patient quality**: AI-referred patients arrive pre-educated and pre-qualified

The impact is measurable and significant. Google AI Overviews is not a future consideration — it is the current state of search visibility for healthcare.

## What This Means for Your Practice

If your practice is not being cited in Google AI Overviews, you are invisible to the majority of patients who search on Google. This is not a theoretical risk — it is a current reality.

The good news is that the technical requirements are well-defined and implementable. Medical schema markup, structured content, and directory consistency are not complex — they just require deliberate effort.

The practices that implement now will be cited in AI Overviews. The practices that wait will be building the same foundation their competitors already have.
