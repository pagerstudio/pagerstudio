LASIK surgery is a high-research, high-consideration decision. Patients spend weeks or months reading about the procedure, comparing clinics, and evaluating surgeons before they book a consultation.

That research is increasingly happening on AI platforms. And for LASIK clinics, this represents both a significant opportunity and an urgent threat.

## The LASIK Patient Research Journey

A typical LASIK patient goes through several stages:

1. **Awareness**: "I'm tired of wearing glasses. What are my options?"
2. **Research**: "Is LASIK safe? What's the difference between LASIK and SMILE?"
3. **Comparison**: "Which LASIK clinic in Bangalore has the best technology?"
4. **Selection**: "Is Dr. [Name] at [Clinic] good for custom LASIK?"
5. **Booking**: "How much does LASIK cost at [Clinic]?"

Stages 1-4 are increasingly happening on AI platforms. ChatGPT answers questions about safety and technology. Perplexity cites sources comparing clinics. Google AI Overviews surfaces structured information about specific practices.

If your LASIK clinic is not present in these answers, you are invisible to patients who are actively trying to choose a provider.

## Why LASIK Is Especially Vulnerable

LASIK has characteristics that make it particularly susceptible to AI visibility competition:

- **Elective procedure**: Patients choose where to go. There is no referral gatekeeper.
- **High research intensity**: Patients compare technology, surgeon experience, and outcomes extensively.
- **Price transparency expectations**: Patients want to know costs before they inquire.
- **Multiple options**: In most cities, there are 5-10 competing LASIK clinics, each with different technology and pricing.

In this environment, the clinic that AI recommends first captures the patient's attention — and often their booking.

## What AI Sees When It Evaluates LASIK Clinics

When a patient asks ChatGPT "best LASIK clinic in Delhi," the AI evaluates:

- **Structured data on your website**: Do you have schema markup identifying your practice, surgeons, and LASIK procedures?
- **Technology specificity**: Do you name your laser platform (e.g., VisuMax, FS200, Schwind AMARIS) or just say "latest technology"?
- **Surgeon credentials**: Are your surgeons named with their qualifications, or do you have a generic "our team" page?
- **Patient outcomes**: Do you have structured outcome data, or just testimonials?
- **Directory presence**: Are you listed consistently on health directories with the same information as your website?

The clinic that provides the most specific, structured, and verifiable information wins the citation.

## The Technology Signal

For LASIK specifically, naming your technology matters more than for most procedures. Patients researching LASIK are sophisticated — they know the difference between a Schwind AMARIS and a VisuMax. When AI can identify your specific technology by name, it can make informed comparisons and cite you in relevant contexts.

This means your website should name:
- Your excimer laser platform
- Your femtosecond laser platform
- Whether you offer wavefront-guided, topography-guided, or SMILE procedures
- Your technology generation (e.g., "10th generation Schwind AMARIS")

Generic claims like "state-of-the-art technology" tell AI nothing. Specific claims tell AI everything it needs to recommend you.

## Building AI Visibility for LASIK

The playbook for LASIK clinics mirrors the broader healthcare AI visibility strategy, with procedure-specific emphasis:

1. **Add MedicalProcedure schema** for each LASIK variant you offer (LASIK, SMILE, PRK, ICL)
2. **Name your technology** on every page — laser models, platforms, and generations
3. **Build comparison content** that answers "LASIK vs SMILE" and "how to choose a LASIK clinic"
4. **Create surgeon profiles** with specific refractive surgery experience and case volumes
5. **Ensure directory consistency** — your clinic name, technology claims, and pricing should be identical across Practo, Google Business Profile, and health directories

## The First-Mover Advantage in LASIK

In most Indian cities, only one or two LASIK clinics are actively building AI visibility. This means the window to establish dominance is open — but it will not stay open forever.

When the first LASIK clinic in a city builds comprehensive AI visibility, they capture a disproportionate share of AI-referred patients. As competitors catch up, the advantage shifts from "being visible" to "being more visible" — and the first mover has a compounding head start.

For LASIK clinics, where procedure volume directly impacts profitability and surgeon experience, this first-mover advantage is worth pursuing aggressively.
