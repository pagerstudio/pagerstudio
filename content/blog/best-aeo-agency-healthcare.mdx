## How to Choose the Right AEO Agency for Your Healthcare Practice

When evaluating AEO agencies, ask these questions:

**1. Do you work exclusively with healthcare?**
Agencies that serve all industries apply general principles. Healthcare requires understanding YMYL standards, medical schema types, and E-E-A-T signals specific to health content.

**2. What AI platforms do you optimize for?**
ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and Gemini each have different citation behaviors. An agency that only optimizes for one platform leaves you invisible on others.

**3. Can you show healthcare-specific schema implementations?**
Medical practices need MedicalClinic, Physician, MedicalProcedure, and FAQPage schema. Generic structured data is not enough.

**4. Do you track AI citations or just traditional rankings?**
Ranking #1 on Google is different from being cited in ChatGPT. Your agency should measure and report on AI citation performance specifically.

**5. What is your approach to E-E-A-T for healthcare?**
AI platforms apply stricter standards to health content. Your agency should understand how to demonstrate Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness for medical content.
